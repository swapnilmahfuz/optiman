# Approximate AI-generated front elevations

Atlas, Noir, Muse and Forma were individually generated from their corresponding existing AI product photographs, inspected, then encoded to 1774×887 alpha WebP at quality 94 without cropping or changing image content. Rims, bridge, materials and rivet styles are distinct. These are approximate frontal synthetic reconstructions, not CAD, fit recommendations or verified alternate product photography. Existing storefront photos remain unchanged.

Optical lens-center alpha is zero for Muse/Forma. Sampled sunglasses lens-center alpha is 207/255 Atlas and 210/255 Noir; backgrounds are alpha zero. No face/mannequin content. Full rim material retains opacity; no global transparency reduction is applied. Clear optical appearance relies on the real face pixels showing through the alpha openings.

The sample portrait is an AI-generated fictional adult with no eyewear. It is labeled as a synthetic example in Photo Preview. It is never presented as a camera feed. No real face/medical data is part of these assets.
